Listening icon: options

The centre orb on the Assistant tab while Boots is listening. Every option reacts to the live mic level (simulated here). Thinking, speaking and no-mic states keep their current look unless you want those changed too.

Current

Bars in a disc

Five bars that grow with your voice. Reads the same as the speaking state, so listening and talking look alike.

Option B

Mic with a level ring

A mic glyph says "listening" at a glance; 48 ticks round the edge rise with your voice, like a radial meter.

Option C

Living blob with eyes

A soft shape that slowly morphs and swells as you speak, with two eyes that blink. Gives Boots a face.

Option D

Mic with ripples

Rings spread out from the mic: one faint ring every 2 s when it's quiet, up to 4 a second, brighter, when you speak. No glow; rings spread less on phone-width screens.
